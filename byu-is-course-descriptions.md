## The IS Junior Core

Per BYU's official IS major flowchart (Information Systems flowchart, Marriott School), the Junior Core is: **IS 402, 404, 415, 403** (fall) plus one of **IS 413, 401, 414, 455** (a required winter "envelope" course — historically one chosen from that group, though your list suggests 401/411/413/414/415 may now all be required rather than a pick-one; confirm current envelope rules with your advisor). Minimum grade B required in core courses; an internship is recommended before starting the core.

## 100–200 Level (Intro / Non-Major)

- **IS 110 — Spreadsheet Skills and Business Analysis**: Hands-on Excel/spreadsheet course covering formulas, functions, pivot tables, and data analysis techniques for solving business problems.
- **IS 111 — Introduction to AI in Business**: A non-technical introduction to how AI tools and concepts are applied in business — common use cases, capabilities, and limitations, no coding required.
- **IS 115 — Introduction to Data Analytics in Python**: Entry-level Python for data analysis — importing, cleaning, and summarizing data, no prior coding experience assumed.
- **IS 120 — Introduction to Web Development**: Fundamentals of building websites — HTML, CSS, and basic JavaScript/interactivity — for students with little or no programming background.
- **IS 199R — Academic Internship: Information Systems**: Supervised, credit-bearing internship in an IS-related role.
- **IS 201 — Introduction to Information Systems**: Broad overview of IS as a field and a major — how technology, business, and people intersect. Common first course for prospective majors.
- **IS 302 — Introduction to Database Design and SQL for Non-Majors**: Relational database design and SQL fundamentals for students outside the IS major.
- **IS 303 — Introduction to Computer Programming**: First programming course — variables, loops, conditionals, functions — for non-majors or as an on-ramp to the major.
- **IS 315 — Introduction to Python Data Analytics (non-majors)**: Python-based data analytics fundamentals for students outside the IS major.
- **IS 355 — Predictive AI (Machine Learning Foundations) for non-majors**: Introduction to predictive modeling/ML concepts and business applications, for non-technical majors.
- **IS 365 — AI in Business**: How organizations apply AI tools and strategy in practice — use cases, decision frameworks, organizational impact.

## 300–400 Level — IS Junior Core & Upper-Division

- **IS 401 — Project Management and Systems Design** *[Catalog-verified]*: "Concepts and skills related to the systems development life cycle, including project management, planning, investigation, requirements definition, and systems design." **(Junior Core)**
- **IS 402 — Database Systems** *[Catalog-verified]*: "Master essential database concepts by learning SQL with a mainstream DBMS, applying normalization to reduce redundancy and errors, and using standard notation to design, model, and demonstrate both conceptual and relational databases." **(Junior Core)**
- **IS 403 — Principles of Business Programming** *[Catalog-verified]*: "Programming business systems. Algorithms, data structures, programming patterns, and interfaces." Focus on C# and ASP.NET MVC web development. **(Junior Core)**
- **IS 404 — Information Technology Infrastructure** *(see title flag above)* *[Catalog-verified, under "Data Communications"]*: "Principles of data communications, local- and wide-area networks, hardware, software, media, standards, management, and business applications." **(Junior Core)**
- **IS 411 — Systems Design and Implementation** *[Catalog-verified]*: "Later phases of systems development life cycle. System architecture, user interfaces, system interface, and application design. Testing and integration. Object-oriented development." **(Junior Core)**
- **IS 413 — Enterprise Application Development** *[Catalog-verified]*: "Client- and server-side programming. Internet and Web-based applications. Enterprise systems and architectures." **(Junior Core)**
- **IS 414 — Information Systems Security and Controls** *[Catalog-verified]*: "Concepts and techniques for securing information systems and applying controls to business processes" — threat identification, prevention/detection, core security principles. **(Junior Core)**
- **IS 415 — Exploratory Data Analysis** *[Catalog-verified]*: "Use the Python programming language in a Jupyter Notebook format to learn the statistics and visualizations required for univariate and bivariate analyses," plus data storytelling and dashboards. **(Junior Core)**
- **IS 455 — Predictive AI in Business (Machine Learning Foundations)**: Foundational machine learning techniques applied to real business problems — natural follow-on to IS 415.
- **IS 456 — Agentic AI in Business**: Building and applying "agentic" (multi-step, autonomous) AI systems in a business context.
- **IS 490R — Mentored Research**: Independent, faculty-mentored research on an IS topic; repeatable, topic varies by student/mentor.

## 500 Level (Advanced Electives & MISM Graduate)

- **IS 501 — Business Information Systems**: Graduate (MISM) foundational IS course for students without an undergraduate IS background.
- **IS 505 — Introduction to Python Data Analytics (non-majors)**: Graduate/MISM equivalent of IS 315.
- **IS 515 — Advanced Spreadsheets for Business Analysis**: Advanced Excel — automation, complex modeling, deeper business analysis techniques.
- **IS 520 — Business Programming and Spreadsheet Automation**: Programming (e.g., macros/VBA-style automation) to automate spreadsheet-based business processes.
- **IS 531 — Enterprise Infrastructure**: Architecture and management of enterprise-scale IT infrastructure — servers, networks, systems supporting large organizations.
- **IS 532 — Cloud Engineering and DevOps**: Cloud platform engineering and DevOps — provisioning cloud infrastructure, CI/CD automation, infrastructure-as-code.
- **IS 533 — Advanced Data Communications**: Deeper, more technical networking — advanced protocols, architecture, enterprise data communications.
- **IS 537 — Data Structures and Algorithms**: Core CS fundamentals — organizing data efficiently, analyzing algorithm performance.
- **IS 540 — Spreadsheet and Database Automation Programming**: Programmatic automation connecting spreadsheets, databases, and custom tooling.
- **IS 542 — Advanced Web Development**: Advanced frameworks and full-stack web application development, building on IS 120/413.
- **IS 543 — Mobile Platform Development**: Building applications for iOS/Android — mobile-specific patterns, tools, deployment.
- **IS 545 — Machine Learning for Business**: ML techniques applied specifically to business problems and decisions.
- **IS 550 — MISM Capstone Introduction**: Introduces the graduate MISM capstone project — scoping and planning.
- **IS 551 — User Experience Design and Technical Leadership**: UX design principles and practice alongside technical leadership skills.
- **IS 552 — MISM Capstone Project**: Culminating applied MISM project, typically team-based for a real client.
- **IS 554 — Investigative and Forensic Data Analysis**: Using data analysis to investigate irregularities, fraud, or anomalies.
- **IS 555 — Data Science Workflows for Organizations**: How data science projects are structured and executed inside real organizations, problem scoping through deployment.
- **IS 556 — Data Engineering**: Building/maintaining data pipelines and infrastructure that feed analytics — ETL, pipelines, data architecture.
- **IS 560 — Information Security Management**: Security from a management/governance angle — policy, risk management, org strategy (complements 414/567).
- **IS 562 — Information Systems Product Management**: Product management for tech/IS products — roadmapping, prioritization, cross-functional leadership.
- **IS 565 — Digital Forensics for Organizations**: Digital forensics practices — investigating security incidents, preserving/analyzing digital evidence.
- **IS 566 — Advanced Database Management**: Advanced database administration, performance tuning, and management beyond IS 402.
- **IS 567 — Cybersecurity and Penetration Testing**: Offensive security — testing systems for vulnerabilities the way an attacker would (complements 414/560).
- **IS 571 — Introduction to Academic Research in Information Systems**: Methods and practice of academic research within IS, for students considering grad study/research.
- **IS 572 — Structural Equation Modeling**: Advanced statistical modeling technique (SEM) used in IS/social-science research.
- **IS 581 — Managing a Software Startup**: Practical challenges of founding/running an early-stage software company.
- **IS 582 — Tech Strategy and Leadership**: Strategic decision-making and leadership in technology organizations.
- **IS 585R — Healthcare Research Hub**: Repeatable research course applying IS methods to healthcare-industry problems.
- **IS 588 — Healthcare Industry Dynamics**: The healthcare industry's structure and challenges, and how IS fits into healthcare organizations.
- **IS 590R — Managing a Software Startup**: Variable-topic seminar; this offering mirrors IS 581.
- **IS 590R — User-Centered Interface Design**: Variable-topic seminar; designing interfaces around real user needs and usability principles.
- **IS 590R — Computer Forensics**: Variable-topic seminar; technical practice of digital/computer forensics investigation.
- **IS 590R — Network Administration**: Variable-topic seminar; hands-on administration of computer networks.
- **IS 590R — Android Mobile Development**: Variable-topic seminar; building applications specifically for Android.
- **IS 590R — Seminar in Information Systems**: Variable-topic seminar covering a current/specialized IS topic, varies by semester.
- **IS 595R — International Experience**: Repeatable course built around an international study/service/business experience.
- **IS 596R — Global Technologies**: How technology is developed, deployed, and used differently across global markets/cultures.
- **IS 599R — Academic Internship: Information Systems**: Graduate/upper-division equivalent of IS 199R.
